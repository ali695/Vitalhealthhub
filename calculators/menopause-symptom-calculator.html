Menopause Symptom Calculator

Menopause is a natural biological transition marking the end of reproductive years, officially defined as 12 consecutive months without a menstrual period. The average age at menopause is 51, though perimenopause (the transitional phase) can begin years earlier. Symptoms vary enormously — some women sail through with minimal discomfort, while others experience significant disruption to daily life.

Most Common Menopause Symptoms

Hot flashes and night sweats affect 75% of women. Sleep disturbances, mood changes, vaginal dryness, cognitive changes (brain fog), joint pain, weight gain around the abdomen, and changes in skin and hair texture are all common. Duration varies from months to over a decade.

Tips to Improve Your Menopause Symptom

  • Track your cycle regularly — irregularities can signal underlying health conditions.
  • Bone density peaks in your 30s — adequate calcium and vitamin D matter from a young age.
  • Hormonal changes affect metabolism — adjust nutrition and exercise plans accordingly.
  • Regular gynecological checkups are essential regardless of age or symptoms.
  • Iron needs are higher during reproductive years — ensure adequate dietary intake.
