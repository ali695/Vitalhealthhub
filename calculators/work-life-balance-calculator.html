How to Use the Work-Life Balance Calculator

  1. Step 1 — Work Hours Per Week: Enter your work hours per week in the input field.
  2. Step 2 — Daily Commute (minutes each way): Enter your daily commute (minutes each way) in the input field.
  3. Step 3 — Vacation Days Per Year: Enter your vacation days per year in the input field.
  4. Step 4 — Exercise Hours Per Week: Enter your exercise hours per week in the input field.
  5. Step 5 — Social/Family Time (hours/week): Enter your social/family time (hours/week) in the input field.
  6. Step 6 — Average Sleep Per Night (hours): Enter your average sleep per night (hours) in the input field.
  7. Step 7 — Click Calculate: Press the Calculate button to instantly get your personalized results.
  8. Step 8 — Read Your Results: Your result will appear color-coded — green for healthy, yellow for borderline, red for at-risk ranges.
  9. Step 9 — Take Action: Use the personalized suggestion in your results to guide your next health steps. Consult a doctor for medical decisions.

Work-Life Balance Calculator

Work-life balance describes the equilibrium between professional responsibilities and personal wellbeing — including rest, relationships, health, and leisure. Chronic imbalance, particularly overwork, is associated with burnout, cardiovascular disease, depression, relationship breakdown, and reduced longevity. The WHO lists overwork as a serious occupational health risk.

Tips to Improve Your Work-Life Balance

  • Small daily habits compound over time — focus on consistency, not perfection.
  • Regular preventive health checkups catch issues before they become serious.
  • Social connections and mental well-being are just as important as physical health.
  • Spend time outdoors — sunlight, fresh air, and nature have measurable health benefits.
  • Adequate sleep (7-9 hours) is foundational to every other aspect of health.
