Pregnancy Weight Gain Calculator

Gaining the right amount of weight during pregnancy is essential for your health and your baby's development. Too little weight gain increases the risk of premature birth and low birth weight. Too much increases the risk of gestational diabetes, C-section, and postpartum weight retention. The recommended range depends on your pre-pregnancy BMI.

Weight Gain by Trimester

In the first trimester, most women gain 1-2 kg total. In the second trimester, gain should be about 0.5 kg per week. The third trimester follows a similar pace. These gains include the baby (3-4 kg at term), placenta (0.7 kg), amniotic fluid (0.8 kg), increased blood volume (1.5 kg), breast tissue (0.9 kg), uterus growth (1 kg), and fat stores (2-4 kg).

Tips to Improve Your Pregnancy Weight Gain

  • Track your cycle regularly — irregularities can signal underlying health conditions.
  • Bone density peaks in your 30s — adequate calcium and vitamin D matter from a young age.
  • Hormonal changes affect metabolism — adjust nutrition and exercise plans accordingly.
  • Regular gynecological checkups are essential regardless of age or symptoms.
  • Iron needs are higher during reproductive years — ensure adequate dietary intake.
