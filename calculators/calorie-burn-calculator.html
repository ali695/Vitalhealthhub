Activity Calorie Burn Calculator

Different activities burn dramatically different numbers of calories. Understanding your calorie burn helps you plan workouts, track activity energy expenditure, and make informed decisions about nutrition. Our calculator uses MET (Metabolic Equivalent of Task) values — a standardized system for measuring exercise intensity used by exercise physiologists worldwide.

Tips to Improve Your Activity Calorie Burn

  • Consistency beats intensity — exercising 4× per week moderately beats 1 exhausting session.
  • Warm up for 5 minutes before training and cool down to prevent injury.
  • Progressive overload — gradually increase weight or reps to keep improving.
  • Rest at least 1-2 days per week for muscle recovery and performance gains.
  • Track your workouts to see long-term progress and stay motivated.
