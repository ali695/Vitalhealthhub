PART 3 — BLOG PAGE STRUCTURE (APPLY TO ALL 100 BLOGS)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Every single blog post must be built with this exact structure:

── 1. FEATURE IMAGE ──
Full width, height 420px desktop / 260px mobile
object-fit: cover, loading="lazy"
alt = exact article title
Unique URL from Part 2 above

── 2. ARTICLE HEADER ──
Category badge (colored pill matching category)
H1 title
Meta row: 
  👤 Ali Haider  |  📅 [publish date]  |  ⏱ [X min read]  |  🔄 Updated [date]
Social share buttons: Twitter, Facebook, LinkedIn, Copy Link

── 3. TABLE OF CONTENTS ──
Auto-generated from all H2 headings on the page
Sticky on desktop (position: sticky, top: 80px)
Smooth scroll on click to each section
Style: background #0d1f17, border-left 3px solid #4caf82
Collapsible on mobile

── 4. INTRODUCTION (150-200 words) ──
Hook sentence that addresses the reader's pain point
Brief overview of what the article covers
1 internal link to a related calculator naturally placed

── 5. MAIN CONTENT BODY ──
Minimum 1200 words per article
Use H2 for main sections, H3 for subsections
Every section minimum 150 words
At least 2 data tables per article (see table specs below)
At least 1 callout/tip box per article
Natural keyword integration throughout
Evidence-based statements only — no vague claims

── 6. EMBEDDED CALCULATOR (most important feature) ──
Every blog must have a relevant embedded calculator widget.
Embed it inline within the content at a natural point — 
not at the top, not at the very bottom, but mid-article 
where it makes contextual sense.

Match calculator to blog topic using this mapping:
Blogs 1-10   (BMI topics)        → Embed BMI Calculator
Blogs 11-22  (Calorie topics)    → Embed Calorie Calculator
Blogs 23-34  (Nutrition/Macros)  → Embed Macro Calculator
Blogs 35-42  (TDEE/Metabolism)   → Embed TDEE Calculator
Blogs 43-50  (Body Fat)          → Embed Body Fat Calculator
Blogs 51-62  (Fitness/Exercise)  → Embed Calorie Burn Calculator
Blogs 63-70  (Sleep)             → Embed Sleep Calculator
Blogs 71-76  (Hydration)         → Embed Water Intake Calculator
Blogs 77-82  (Heart Rate)        → Embed Heart Rate Calculator
Blogs 83-100 (Wellness/General)  → Embed the most relevant calculator
                                    based on article content

Calculator embed implementation:
- Use an <iframe> or component embed pointing to the actual 
  calculator page on the site
- Wrap in a styled container:
  background: #0d1f17
  border: 1px solid #2d7a4f
  border-radius: 12px
  padding: 24px
  margin: 40px 0
- Add heading above: "Try It Yourself: [Calculator Name]"
  style: color #4caf82, font-size 1.1rem, margin-bottom 12px
- Add caption below: "Use this free calculator to get your 
  personalized results instantly."

── 7. DATA TABLES (minimum 2 per article) ──
Every table must be styled:
- Wrapper: overflow-x: auto (mobile scroll)
- border-radius: 10px, overflow: hidden
- Header row: background #1e3a2f, color #c8e6d4, font-weight 600
- Alternating rows: #0f1a17 and #131f18, color #9ec8b0
- All cells: padding 12px 16px, border-bottom 1px solid #1a3028
- Status badges where applicable:
  Healthy → background #1a3d28, color #4caf82
  Borderline → background #3d3a1a, color #cfc84c  
  At Risk → background #3d1a1a, color #cf4c4c

Example tables to include per category:
BMI blogs: BMI classification table, healthy weight by height table
Calorie blogs: calorie needs by age/gender, calorie content of foods
Nutrition blogs: macro ratios for different goals, protein content table
TDEE blogs: activity multipliers table, BMR by age table
Body fat blogs: body fat % ranges by age/gender
Fitness blogs: exercise calorie burn table, muscle group workout table
Sleep blogs: recommended sleep by age, sleep stages duration table
Hydration blogs: water needs by weight/activity, hydrating foods table
Heart rate blogs: heart rate zones table, resting HR ranges table
Wellness blogs: habit tracking table, health metrics reference table

── 8. KEY TAKEAWAYS BOX ──
Place just before the conclusion section
Style: background #0d1f17, border-left 4px solid #4caf82
       border-radius 8px, padding 20px 24px
Heading: "Key Takeaways" in #4caf82
4-5 bullet points summarizing the most important insights
Each bullet: concise, actionable, evidence-based

── 9. CONCLUSION (100-150 words) ──
Summarize main points in 2-3 sentences
Soft CTA: "Ready to find your numbers? Try our free 
[relevant calculator] →" linking to the calculator page
Encouraging, positive closing tone

── 10. FAQ SECTION (5 questions) ──

── 11. RELATED ARTICLES SECTION ──
