--------------------------------------------------
RULES FOR META DESCRIPTIONS
--------------------------------------------------

Each description must:

- include primary keyword naturally
- include benefit-driven copy

DESCRIPTION COPYWRITING TECHNIQUES

- emotional triggers (control, confidence, improvement)

Check your BMI instantly and understand your health status. Get personalized insights to take control of your fitness journey today.

IMPORTANT RULES
